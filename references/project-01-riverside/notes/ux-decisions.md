# UX Decisions

## Decision 1: Find Doctors is for comparison

Doctor listing should support comparison, not full scheduling.

## Decision 2: Doctor Profile supports slot selection

Users choose date/time after reviewing the doctor.

## Decision 3: Booking confirms the selected appointment

Booking page collects patient details and reviews selected slot.

## Decision 4: Confirmation reduces uncertainty

Confirmation includes reference number, summary, next steps, and useful actions.

## Decision 5: Manage Appointment gives control

Users can look up, reschedule, or cancel.
